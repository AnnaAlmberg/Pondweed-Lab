Meet Elodea

Elodea canadensis (Canadian pondweed) is a submerged freshwater flowering plant, native to North America. In Australia it is an introduced water weed that spreads from broken stem fragments. Its leaves are only two cell layers thick and have no stomata, so light reaches every chloroplast and gases diffuse directly into the leaf cells. The oxygen released by photosynthesis escapes from the cut stem as bubbles, which makes Elodea a model organism for measuring the rate of photosynthesis. Click a picture to see it larger.
